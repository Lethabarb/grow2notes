# Word-template set-up list

What a manager enters so that Grow2Notes offers the provider's Word support-note template: the common item groups and
their items, in the template's order. It is used by the managers' production set-up
([F06.05](../design/backlog/E06-hardening-and-go-live/F06.05-production-set-up.md)) and by the `/onboard-provider`
skill ([onboard-provider.md](onboard-provider.md)), whose `template.csv` takes each item as a "Common item" row, or a
"Common item with details" row for an item marked **needs detail** below. It follows D104 and the assumed defaults A145
to A147 (design.md §13).

## What this file holds, and what it leaves out

- **Only the template's fixed wording**, from the owner's parity report of 10 November 2026 and the requirements it
  lists (R25 to R29, R33, R34, R36, R38, R40). It never holds a participant's name, a goal, a Medication line or a note.
- **Quoted word for word** wording has no mark. Anything else carries **Wording to confirm against the Word template**:
  the report summarises some of the template instead of quoting it, so those items, and every group name or item list
  that the report does not give, must be checked against a Word day before they are entered. Nothing here is guessed
  into a final form; a line marked to confirm is a placeholder for what the template says.
- **Venue names are placeholders.** Where the template names a venue, enter the provider's own name for it.
- **Not set up:** the frequency choice (occasionally, regularly, continuously), which D104 did not choose; the
  placement-specific and participant-specific variants of a section (R39), such as a café set or "apron" for "gloves";
  and the wording variants that crept into single days (for example "No prompts required"). The template holds one
  version of each item.
- **Not items:** the **Medication** line is a switch on each participant (a manager's choice, not part of this list),
  the goals are each participant's own, and **Daily Support Notes** is the Guided notes box, so none appears below.

## The rules this list follows

1. Each Word section is one **group**, named with its Word heading. Bracketed purpose tags stay in the name (A145).
2. A sub-heading is its own group, named `<section>: <sub-heading>` (A145).
3. A parent line with child options is several items, each worded with its option, for example "Assistance required
   when using the microwave" (A146).
4. A travel section names its venue in the group name. The pick-up and drop-off options and the 11 Further Supports
   are groups like any other (A147).
5. Group order is organisation-wide: enter the groups in the order below, and the items in the order given (A147).
6. An item marked **needs detail** asks for a short text when it is ticked on a note, required to submit (A151).
7. The names and items are at most 200 characters each.

The daily report prints every group, picked or not, with the unpicked ones empty (A161). A long list therefore makes a
long page for every participant. Enter only the groups that the provider wants on every participant's page; the
activities that few people do can be left out until the owner has seen a busy day (first-use step 225).

## The groups, in Word order

The order of the groups is the one in which the report summarises a block: the pick-up and drop-off options, then the
travel and activity sections, then Further Supports. The activities appear in different orders on different days.
**Wording to confirm against the Word template:** the order of the groups from Morning Tea and Lunch to Activities
of choice.

### Pick-up and drop-off

The group's name is **Wording to confirm against the Word template**. Four options, in this order:

1. Collected from/dropped home by CSW
2. Dropped/picked up at `<base venue>` by parent/external support/family friend/other (`<base venue>` is a
   placeholder: enter the provider's own venue name)
3. Collected from/dropped to station
4. Other (staff to complete if different from above) (**needs detail**)

### G2B Bus Travel (Goal Focused Support, Life Skills Development)

"G2B" may be the provider's own name for its bus, so it is **Wording to confirm against the Word template** (and with
the owner) wherever it appears: this heading, item 7 and the venue travel items below. Seven items, in this order:

1. No prompting required
2. Reminded to use the bathroom if needed before we leave
3. Prompted seatbelts
4. Reminded of shared space etiquette
5. Reminded of respectful interactions and using manners
6. Reminded of the bus routine – fill in from the back/get off from the front
7. Physical support to get into/out of a vehicle (G2B bus or CSW vehicle)

### The venue travel groups

Four headings, each with the venue as a placeholder (`<venue>`). Enter one group for each venue a participant travels
to or from, with the venue's own name in the heading.

- Private vehicle travel to/from `<venue>`
- Private vehicle OR G2B bus to/from `<venue>`
- Bus travel from `<venue>`
- Private vehicle to `<venue>` (no purpose tags)

Items quoted for these headings, in no order given:

- Prompted to put bag in the boot
- Prompted seat belt safety
- Reminded to treat CSW's car with care and respect (do not slam doors or scrape bags)
- Physical support to get into/out of a vehicle (G2B bus or CSW vehicle)

**Wording to confirm against the Word template:** which of these four items belong under which heading (the report says
the headings have 4 to 6 items each), the other items of each heading, whether a heading carries purpose tags, and the
order of the items.

Each is one group, named with its Word heading and its bracketed purpose tags. The tags come from this list: Goal

template**; enter each from a Word day.

- Activities of choice (it appears on all five days)
- Photography Out and About
- Woodwork, and Woodwork - `<site>`, which has a different tool list (two groups; `<site>` is a placeholder)
- `<venue>` Art (`<venue>` is a placeholder: use the provider's own venue name)
- Walk around the lake (no purpose tags)
- Walk and Outdoor Gym (no purpose tags), with its tagged sub-heading as its own group, Walk and Outdoor Gym: Fitness
  Park
- Baking (no purpose tags)
- Market Stall @ `<venue>` (no purpose tags; `<venue>` is a placeholder: use the provider's own venue name)
- Bowling (two purpose tags)

entered once in each group that has them. The words of each parent are **Wording to confirm against the Word
template**.

## After entering it

- Check the groups and items against a Word day, and write down every line marked to confirm that did not match
  (first-use step 229).
- Switch the Medication box on for the participants who have a medication arrangement at the program, in each
  participant's Details; the skill never does (SKILL.md rule 7).
- Set each goal's term when the goals are entered (Short term unless the Word template puts the goal under Medium or
  Long term goal).
