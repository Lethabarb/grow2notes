# F07.05 Hardening, manual tests and switch-on

**Outcome:** the server is rate-limited, checked against its security design and tried with at least two different
approved assistants. The provider has what it needs to approve assistants, tell people and amend the hosting
agreement, including the operator's check of its own AI provider. Once every switch-on line has its owner and evidence,
`Mcp:Enabled` is turned on in production
([mcp-server.md §11.1](../../mcp-server.md#111-switch-on-checklist-release-2-per-environment),
[README §9](../README.md#9-definition-of-done) *Release*, R2).

### Notes

  nothing, and previews and forms do not count
  (`Confirmed_invites_resets_and_deactivations_allow_10_an_hour_for_each_user`, `ConfirmedChangeLimitTests`).
  `/oauth/token` and `/oauth/revoke` are limited for each approved client ID, with an overall cap and one small shared
  bucket (`The_token_and_revocation_endpoints_allow_30_for_each_assistant_10_for_the_rest_and_60_overall`).
  `/oauth/authorize` has its own count at the sign-in number, and `/api/connect` is in the sign-in group
  (`The_authorise_endpoint_and_the_consent_endpoints_allow_30_a_minute_for_each_address`). A139 records the choices.
