# F07.05 Hardening, manual tests and switch-on

**Outcome:** the server is rate-limited, checked against its security design and tried with at least two different
approved assistants. The provider has what it needs to approve assistants, tell people and amend the hosting
agreement, including the operator's check of its own AI provider. Once every switch-on line has its owner and evidence,
`Mcp:Enabled` is turned on in production
([mcp-server.md §11.1](../../mcp-server.md#111-switch-on-checklist-release-2-per-environment),
[README §9](../README.md#9-definition-of-done) *Release*, R2).

[mcp-server.md §11.1](../../mcp-server.md#111-switch-on-checklist-release-2-per-environment), linked and not copied.
Lines the provider or the owner does carry no story and no points; they are ticked here with their evidence
([README §9](../README.md#9-definition-of-done) *Short variants*). Only the operator's work on a line is a pointed
story. The checklist is per environment; this table is for production. The test environment holds made-up data only,
and any assistant may be registered there (§11.6).

  and 4).

      client share one small bucket.
- [ ] `/oauth/authorize` and the consent endpoints are in the existing sign-in group.
