# F06.06 Go-live readiness and cut-over

**Outcome:** every line of the go-live checklist has an owner and evidence, the operator's developer work on those
lines is done, and every staff member switches from the Word template to Grow2Notes on one set date, with no double
entry ([design.md §14 Go-live checklist](../../design.md#go-live-checklist), A40).

## Stories
- S06.06.01 Breach response runbook
- S06.06.02 Restore test of the production backup
- S06.06.03 Cut-over on the set date

## Go-live checklist
The lines below are the go-live checklist in [design.md §14](../../design.md#go-live-checklist), linked and not
copied. Lines the provider or the owner does carry no story and no points; they are ticked here with their evidence
([README §9](../README.md#9-definition-of-done) *Short variants*). Only the operator's developer work on a line is a
pointed story.

Notes on the lines:
- **Line 4:** the clauses in the line are suggestions for the owner and a lawyer to settle. The AI provider clauses
  (D56, D57) belong to Release 2's switch-on checklist (mcp-server.md §11.1, feature F07.05), not to this line.
- **Line 1:** the R1 privacy policy names Microsoft Azure only. Naming an AI provider is part of Release 2's switch-on
  checklist.
- **Line 6:** the session covers the topics in the line, including that incidents still go on the existing incident
  form (D16) and nothing needs saving on the device (D22). passkey-flows.md *Per-screen notes* adds explaining
  cross-device passkeys here, not on screen.
- **Line 7:** the checking manager previews the guide prompts as settings-textarea.md *Per-screen notes* describes.
- **Lines 9 and 10:** the gathered Word notes are the record for dates before go-live, under the same retention rules
  (A39; design.md §14 M6 *Migration from Word*). They are not imported.

Enabler: `ops/breach-response.md`, prepared in advance as design.md §9.10 asks. So that, if a breach is suspected, the
managers and the operator know who decides, what to do first and what to keep, and the provider can complete its
30-day assessment.

### Acceptance criteria
Already specified elsewhere (linked, not copied):
- [design.md §9.10](../../design.md#910-breach-response-notifiable-data-breaches-scheme): *The legal position*,
  *Contain*, *Work out what was exposed*, *Preserve evidence* and *Prepare in advance*.
- [design.md §7.4](../../design.md#74-operator-commands): `admin signout-all`.

Specific to this story:
- [ ] The runbook names who decides (the managers), who supports (the operator, with its contact details), and the
      OAIC notification form, and has a table for decisions and dates.
- [ ] Containment steps: deactivate affected accounts, run `admin signout-all`, and stop the web app if needed.
- [ ] To work out what was exposed it points to the audit extraction runbook (S05.04.02) and the Azure Activity Log,
      and says a misused account is assumed to have exposed every participant's notes, because reads are not audited
      (A28).
- [ ] To preserve evidence it says to export the relevant audit rows and logs before any clean-up, and points to the
      restore drill runbook (S06.03.02) for a point-in-time copy.
- [ ] It says the assessment and any notification are the provider's, and that the operator tells the provider
      without delay.
- [ ] The operator's contact details are given to the provider for its own plan (checklist line 3).
- [ ] A manager has read it; their name and the date are in the pull request.

Enabler: the first restore of the latest production backup, run with the restore drill runbook and recorded. So that
the backups are known to work before staff rely on Grow2Notes as their only record.

### Acceptance criteria
Already specified elsewhere (linked, not copied):
- [design.md §14 Go-live checklist](../../design.md#go-live-checklist): the *Restore test* line.
- [design.md §14 M6 *Done when*](../../design.md#m6-hardening-and-go-live): a restore test has been done and
  recorded.
- [design.md §10.7](../../design.md#107-backups-and-tested-restore-a36) *Restore drill*.

Specific to this story:
- [ ] The operator follows `ops/restore-drill.md` exactly, as the Entra admin, restoring the latest production backup
      to a scratch database on the production server.
- [ ] `ops/restore-check.sql` passes, with `DBCC CHECKDB` clean, and a known note and its history open from the copy
      (see the owner question in releases.md on when production first has a note).
- [ ] The date, restore point, result and time taken are recorded in `ops/restore-log.md`, and the time is compared
      with the 4-hour target.
- [ ] The copy is deleted and its deletion is recorded.
- [ ] Any step in the runbook that did not work as written is corrected in the same pull request.

Enabler: the production release on an agreed date and the record of the switch-over. So that every staff member moves
from the Word template to Grow2Notes on the same day, with no double entry (A40).

### Acceptance criteria
Already specified elsewhere (linked, not copied):
- [design.md §14 M6](../../design.md#m6-hardening-and-go-live) *Cut-over*, *Migration from Word* and *Done when*.
- [design.md §10.4](../../design.md#104-cicd-with-github-actions) `deploy.yml`: production only by
  `workflow_dispatch`, naming the CI run of a build that passed in test; the smoke tests.
- [README §9](../README.md#9-definition-of-done) *Release*, R1.

Specific to this story:
- [ ] The owner and the managers agree the switch-over date, and the production deploy runs at an agreed time outside
      the usual note-writing hours (A35).
- [ ] The build in production is the CI run that passed in test, and the deploy's smoke tests pass.
- [ ] On the switch-over morning, production holds participants, goals, common items in their groups and guide
      prompts, and no notes (design.md §14 M6 *Migration from Word*).
- [ ] Every line of the go-live checklist above has its owner and evidence.
- [ ] The switch-over date is recorded in [releases.md](../releases.md) and in checklist line 10.

### Notes
- A short pilot with a few workers before the set date is available if the owner wants one (A40). It is not in this
  backlog unless the owner asks for it.
