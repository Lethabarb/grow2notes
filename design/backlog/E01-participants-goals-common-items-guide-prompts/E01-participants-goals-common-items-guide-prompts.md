# E01 Participants, goals, common items and guide prompts

**Goal:** managers set up participants and each participant's goals, the organisation's common items in groups (with
the built-in Every note group first and fixed) and the guide prompts, on a phone or a laptop; workers cannot reach any
of it, nothing can be deleted, and every change is audited with its old and new values.
