# F05.03 Archive and deactivate end to end

**Outcome:** with every screen, report and export now built, archiving a participant, goal, common item or group, and
deactivating a user, behave exactly as [design.md §3.7](../../design.md#37-archiving-nothing-is-deleted) says
everywhere at once: new work stops, and every existing record stays as it was.

## Stories
- S05.03.01 Archiving keeps every past record unchanged
- S05.03.02 A deactivated person is signed out everywhere and their notes stay

## Feature checks
This feature owns this row in [coverage.md](../coverage.md) (E05). It is linked, not copied:
- design.md §14 M5 *Scope*: archive and deactivate flows checked end to end.

The archive, restore, deactivate and reactivate actions themselves, and their screens, belong to E00 and E01
(participants.md › *Archive and Restore*, common-items.md › *Archive and restore*, users.md › *Detail and actions*).
These stories check the effects across the whole app and fix any gap they find.

As a manager, I want archiving a participant, goal, common item or group to stop it being used for new notes while
every past note, report and export stays exactly as it was, so that archiving never loses or alters a record.

### Acceptance criteria
Already specified elsewhere (linked, not copied):
- [design.md §3.7](../../design.md#37-archiving-nothing-is-deleted): the table rows for Goal, Common item, Common item
  group and Participant, and the rule that nothing is deleted.
- [participants.md › Acceptance criteria › Archive and Restore](../../ux/screens/participants.md#acceptance-criteria)
  and [common-items.md › Acceptance criteria › Archive and restore](../../ux/screens/common-items.md#acceptance-criteria)
  (owned by E01; used here as the starting point).

Specific to this story (end-to-end tests in the test environment, made-up data):
- [ ] Given a participant with submitted notes and a draft started today, when a manager archives them: they leave
      Today and search; a worker's and a manager's attempt to start a note for any date gets
      `422 note.participant_archived`; the author can still submit the existing draft.
- [ ] After that archive, the participant's history, the text of the daily report for a past date and the text of a
      record export are the same as before it, apart from the "As at" and export time stamps.
- [ ] After restore, the participant is back on Today and a new note can be started.
- [ ] Given a goal, a common item and a group each archived after a draft was started, the draft and every earlier
      version keep them; a note started afterwards does not show them; reports and exports of earlier notes still
      show them (A3).
- [ ] A restored goal, item or group returns at the end of its list (A2); the Every note group cannot be archived
      (A43).
- [ ] Any gap found is fixed in this story, with a test.

As a manager, I want a person I deactivate to be signed out on every device at once while their notes keep their
name, so that someone who has left loses access straight away and no record changes.

### Acceptance criteria
Already specified elsewhere (linked, not copied):
- [design.md §3.7](../../design.md#37-archiving-nothing-is-deleted) User row, and
  [§8.6](../../design.md#86-deactivation-reset-and-recovery) *Deactivate*.
- [users.md › Acceptance criteria › Detail and actions](../../ux/screens/users.md#acceptance-criteria--testable-bullets-a-developer-and-tester-can-check) (owned by
  E00; used here as the starting point).

Specific to this story (end-to-end tests in the test environment, made-up data):
- [ ] Given a worker signed in on a phone and a laptop (two browser contexts) with a draft open, when a manager
      deactivates them, the next request on each device gets `401` and shows the signed-out-in-place view.
- [ ] Signing in again is refused with the same generic message for both setup paths (passkey, and password with a
      6-digit code).
- [ ] Their submitted notes still show their name in the read view, Past notes, the daily report and a record export.
- [ ] Their open draft stays until a manager discards it from the read view; after that, a new note for that
      participant and day can be started (A9).
- [ ] After reactivation they sign in with their existing method, and `user.deactivated` and `user.reactivated` are
      in the audit log.
- [ ] Any gap found is fixed in this story, with a test.
